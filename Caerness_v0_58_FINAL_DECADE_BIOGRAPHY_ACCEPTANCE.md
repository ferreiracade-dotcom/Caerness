## What actually happened to the six people

- He **did get into gang fights**. Four collection disputes became real street fights. Two ended without a clean territorial winner; twice his crew held the collection point and improved its local position.
- His gang did **not** run from a restaurant in this branch. It used the **Aldergate ward cartage yard** as a legitimate business, meeting point, storage point and payment point for the crew. The front remained active and ended with 197.0 cash, 47.5 reputation and 2.0 debt.

- By Year 10 the engine classifies the operation as an **eligible emergent criminal-organization candidate** (significance 61.0), rather than silently attaching Cale to a pre-existing family.
- He suffered no persisted personal injury in this branch and formed no tracked household/union. This is a criminal-career biography, not a forced family arc.

## Mechanics accepted in v0.58

1. **No census balancing.** Stage counts can diverge from World Bible baselines. Sixth is not refilled toward 40; a quiet decade with zero new Sixths is legal.
2. **Summit remains permeable.** A causal regression using fully ready Fifths plus genuine Stage-bound summit problems produced 5 new Sixths without any demographic multiplier.
3. **Concrete experiences are one-use and Stage-bound.** A tournament bout/challenge can inform one genuine check; it cannot be rerolled indefinitely or carried forward across a Stage transition.
4. **Lethal challenges now mean lethal.** If an accepted lethal duel resolves with a winner, the loser is marked deceased.
5. **DORMANT ≠ individually narrated.** DORMANT lives continue through cohort, household, economy, institution and background owners. STRATEGIC/TRACKED promotion increases resolution detail, not probability privilege.
6. **Underworld orders resolve.** Targeted killings no longer remain perpetual labels; they can succeed, fail, or expose the crew and persist consequences.
7. **Role duties are specific enough to support biography.** Retainer and institutional service now selects concrete duty forms instead of a generic “assignment happened” summary.
