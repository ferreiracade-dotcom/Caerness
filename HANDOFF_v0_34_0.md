## Purpose
Add causal ordinary-life consequence bridges without generating activity merely to populate tables.

## Added behavior
- Sponsor-loss guardianship: a dependent child/ward whose sponsor dies can be assigned an already-existing living adult in the same household, prioritising recognized parent/adoptive parent, sibling, household head, then other household adults. No remote kin are invented.
- Household service: characters may seek service only from quantitatively resolved households that have real staffing capacity and liquidity. Structural-only households are excluded.
- Household migration: a genuinely declining quantitative household may schedule movement only over a real usable route toward an objectively stronger adjacent location. Structural-only households are excluded.
- Credit initiation: a cash-stressed owned enterprise may seek credit only from a real local loan-house enterprise with transferable cash. Accepted credit conserves cash between lender and borrower.
- Credit service: borrowers with cash can make real payments that reduce outstanding debt.
