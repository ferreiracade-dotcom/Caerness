## Immediate next slice — INTEGRATION-01 health + agriculture/weather + law/procedure

The remaining large World Bible domains should now be integrated before the first fully covered one-year unattended run. This should be one bounded integration phase with three domain owners, not one giant rewrite.

### A. HEALTH-01 persistent condition course + treatment capacity

Current health already owns conditions and WAR now creates real battle injuries. Deepen the owner so ordinary illness/injury and war consequences can progress causally.

Target:

```text
injury / illness / childbirth / channel condition
→ condition state + severity
→ symptoms/capacity impact
→ treatment access/capacity
→ treatment choice
→ recovery / chronic limitation / disability / death
→ household/work/institution consequences
```

Guardrails:

- condition progression must not magically know a physician is available;
- physicians/institutions own diagnosis/treatment capacity;
- cultivation can alter prognosis without becoming universal regeneration;
- aggregate epidemics and individual condition courses remain separate resolution problems.

Required bounded test: fracture or serious wound with limited healer capacity, including a case where delayed/unavailable treatment materially changes outcome.

### B. AGRI-01 regional harvest/weather causal loop

Create a real agriculture/weather owner sufficient to connect food production to the systems that now depend on it.

Target:

```text
weather/season/land/labor/input conditions
→ crop/harvest result
→ physical food/stores
→ market supply/price
→ household food stress
→ tax/public finance
→ migration/crime
→ army provisioning
```

War must be able to requisition, burn or disrupt actual harvest/stores through this owner rather than a generic war-damage modifier.

Required bounded test: one poor harvest should propagate into food availability/price and at least several real downstream owners without a scripted `famine += 1` chain.

### C. LAW-01 rules/jurisdiction/procedure

Add the minimum general law owner required by the World Bible and the now-richer economy/underworld/state systems.

Target:

```text
applicable jurisdiction/rule
→ complaint / administrative trigger
→ evidence/records
→ office/official capacity + delay
→ hearing/decision where required
→ judgment/remedy/enforcement
→ property/contract/person/institution consequence
→ information/reputation response
```

The law layer should resolve which authority applies (imperial/kingdom/charter/local/Churn-specific where canon permits) rather than every subsystem embedding its own legal if-statements.

Required bounded test: a concrete commercial/property or criminal matter must pass through jurisdiction, evidence, delay, decision and enforcement; absence/corruption/capacity limits must be able to stall or distort the process.

## After INTEGRATION-01 — COVERAGE-01 cross-system gate

Perform a fresh World Bible → engine pass. Every major domain must have:

```text
authoritative owner
persistent state or explicit aggregate representation
autonomous process
cross-domain outputs
coverage reporting
resolution behavior
belief/competence boundary where relevant
```

The gate must identify any system that merely has tables/functions but never actually runs or affects the world.

Also prove promotion/down-resolution:

- latent/background person can crystallize when events make them important;
- ordinary merchant can become regionally significant;
- new gang/Mafia family/institution can become tracked when scale warrants;
- once-rich entities can safely down-resolve when no longer relevant;
- no player/focus privilege in those rules.

## Then — integrated ordinary one-year unattended run

Run one ordinary year with every major domain active at least at its appropriate resolution.

The one-year run should include the **possibility**, not the guarantee, of:

- births/deaths/marriage/household change;
- training/cultivation progression;
- business creation/closure/trade/credit/production;
- public finance/projects/migration;
- underworld formation/escalation;
- political succession/conflict;
- illness/injury/treatment;
- harvest/weather shocks;
- legal/administrative procedure;
- war/campaign consequences;
- information/rumour/reputation;
- crystallization/down-resolution.

A run is not accepted merely because it completes. Coverage telemetry must prove the domains actually ran and cross-system causal effects occurred where conditions warranted them.

## Only then — ten-year world run

The ten-year run is the long-horizon validation of an already-covered world, not the mechanism for discovering that half the systems were dormant.

Check:

- demographic/institutional continuity;
- Stage ecology without quotas;
- regional divergence;
- business and underworld turnover;
- political succession and war recovery;
- debt/property/inheritance continuity;
- culture/information change;
- promotion/down-resolution stability;
- performance/database growth;
- causal explainability.

## Architecture rule

> From here, new work should primarily close missing causal owners and prove cross-system interaction. Do not add another broad framework or detached subsystem unless a concrete World Bible capability cannot be represented by the current architecture.
