## Immediate next slice — WAR-01 campaign → battle → world consequences

The engine already has battle resolution and an earlier military-logistics/aftermath layer.
WAR-01 should now close the remaining world-integration gap: war must not end at a battle row or
an army-readiness change.

### Target chain

```text
political objective / conflict
→ mobilization and actual campaign
→ route/supply movement
→ battle / siege / raid at appropriate resolution
→ casualties, injury, capture, desertion, equipment/supply loss
→ property/infrastructure/agricultural damage
→ refugees / displaced households / dependants
→ vacancies, succession and institutional consequences
→ market, tax and labor consequences
→ official reports / eyewitness claims / rumours
→ BELIEF-01 updates
→ later political / military / household / economic decisions
```

No `war_damage += 10` should substitute for these consequences where real owners exist.

### Reuse existing systems

Prefer existing owners:

- `war.py` campaign logistics/readiness/aftermath;
- `combat.py` / `battlefield.py` for engagements;
- ecology/logistics for routes and supply;
- health for persistent injuries/disability;
- household life for death/dependants/displacement;
- property/buildings and GROWTH public infrastructure for physical damage;
- MERCHANT/CRAFT/civilian for lost stock, production and commercial shock;
- politics for casualties among office-holders and succession pressure;
- information/BELIEF for incompatible reports and delayed knowledge;
- DEC-01 for commanders/rulers acting only from information they possess;
- EVENTS-01 pattern where a strategic campaign owner benefits from replay/audit.

### Required WAR-01 acceptance scenario

Use one bounded campaign with at least two sides and a real route/supply chain.

Acceptance should prove:

1. army supply comes from persisted capacity/stock and route access rather than teleportation;
2. a blocked route or lost depot can create a real shortage;
3. a battle consumes/changes persistent people and material state;
4. named casualties/injuries survive the battle resolver into health/household/institution state;
5. aggregate casualties can remain aggregate until crystallization is warranted;
6. damaged property/route/stock changes downstream civilian/economic state;
7. displaced households/refugees move through real geography where crystallized;
8. political offices/institutions react to actual vacancies rather than scripted drama;
9. at least two information versions of the battle can exist (official and eyewitness/rumour), with actors knowing only received versions;
10. aftermath persists after combat ends and can affect readiness, finance and local recovery;
11. no protagonist privilege;
12. old saves migrate without fabricated wars, casualties or refugee histories.

### Resolution rule

War must use the same resolution ladder as the rest of Caerness.

- Low-relevance distant engagements may resolve through aggregate unit/formation state.
- Named/high-relevance people persist individually where already crystallized.
- A cultivator's contribution depends on their actual military task and condition, not a flat Stage bonus.
- Consequences may promote latent people/institutions to richer tracking when they become historically important.

## After WAR-01

1. Health + agriculture/weather + law/procedure integration.
2. Cross-system World-Bible coverage/regression gate.
3. Integrated ordinary one-year unattended run with every major domain active and resolution promotion/down-resolution functioning.
4. Ten-year world run only after that one-year gate passes.

## Architecture rule

> A war is not a minigame. It is a temporary concentration of the same people, goods, roads,
> institutions, information, health, households and money that exist before and after the fighting.
