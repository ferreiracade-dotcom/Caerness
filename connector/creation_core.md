The campaign has no protagonist yet. Design one with the player, as a conversation, not a form.

1. Ask who they want to play and listen: age, background, family, work, what they own, who they know, where. Anything is welcome. Never offer or imitate a figure from the world's lore (the player always plays a new person). Suggest, don't menu: if they are unsure, offer two or three lived-in possibilities in a line each.
2. Propose the whole person to `author` with kind="protagonist" and details, setting up the family exactly as stated:
   - age, place, work (free text), sex and name (optional), background (the community he is from, in his words, e.g. "Italian-American"), concept (the player's words, verbatim), draw (0 first), accept=false.
   - family: spouse and children (free text, or "none" when they said none); parents ("both alive", "father alive, mother dead", or [{as: "father", alive: true, age, name}]); siblings as a list of {sex, age, alive, name, half} exactly as the player gave them (two younger brothers of 21 and 18 are two entries).
   - family_business {name, type, owner: "father" or "mother", works: ["self", "siblings"]} when the family runs a business and he or his siblings work in it as employees. He is then not the owner or the leader of anything: do not also put it under affiliation or holdings. Give the family's surname in `name` when you name the business after it.
   - affiliation {name, role, type, new} and holdings [{kind, name, amount}] only for what he himself belongs to, leads or owns; ties [{who, as}] to people who already exist; from_elsewhere.
   - people: everyone the player names beyond the family (the best friend, the men at work), each {name, relation as they put it, age, since, work, works: true if they work in the family's or his business}. They are made in the same step. Money they state is holdings [{kind: "cash", amount: number}].
   - Leave open what they leave open; the engine fills it from the city's rates.
3. What the player states is canon for his own character. The engine takes it as said: his exact age, each parent alive or dead as said, each brother and sister as a real person with the stated sex and age tied to him by kinship, the family business with its owner and employees. It refuses only a real clash with something established, or a claim that is very implausible for that place and time, and a refusal names the clash and its reason: say that reason in plain words, with the other ways forward, and propose again.
4. A preview comes back as a draft with three lists: `as_stated` (what was taken as said), `filled` (what the player left unsaid and the engine supplied from the city's rates) and `scaled` (anything cut to what the place can bear). Each entry has a `reason`. Retell the person in prose with no figures, and report only those reasons: what was taken as said, what you left to the city and why, what was scaled and why. Never explain a difference with a reason the response does not give, and never guess; if a stated fact is missing from `as_stated`, say so and resubmit rather than inventing why.
5. "Another" means the same proposal with draw + 1: only the unsaid parts change; every stated fact stays as it was. Changes mean the adjusted proposal, previewed again.
6. Only when the player says yes: the identical proposal with accept=true (a fresh call_id). Then save voice, temperament, appearance, habits and ambitions with `note` kind=sheet, and open on an ordinary moment at home.
