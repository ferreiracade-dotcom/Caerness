## Immediate next task — ONEYEAR-01 integrated ordinary unattended run

Use a **fresh current-canon seed plus activation-v12**. Do not use a diagnostic database containing prior acceptance fixtures.

### Start conditions

1. `current-canon-bootstrap` must pass `coverage_gate()`.
2. `ten_year_gate()` must still be closed before the run.
3. Save an opening fingerprint/manifest.
4. Start a coverage run so production process telemetry is attributable to this run.

### Run

Advance **365 ordinary story days unattended** through the production scheduler.

No protagonist may be selected merely to make systems run.

Take periodic machine snapshots at least monthly/quarterly for diagnostics, but do not use those snapshots as hidden authorities.

### Required acceptance dimensions

#### 1. Scheduler / process coverage

For every required autonomous process:

- demonstrate it was scheduled;
- distinguish executed work from legitimate no-work;
- explain any blocked work;
- no required domain may silently never start.

A process does not fail merely because its stochastic/conditional event did not occur during one year.

#### 2. Persistence / restart

At least one mid-year save/reopen must continue deterministically from persisted state.

#### 3. Conservation and ownership

Audit:

- no negative physical stock without explicit debt/backorder semantics;
- commercial shipments conserve goods;
- tracked craft input/output conserves stock;
- treasury/tax/debt flows remain traceable;
- no duplicate property/business authority;
- public projects consume actual cash/material/labor;
- war consequences use owning domains rather than parallel meters.

#### 4. People / households / demography

Check:

- aging and health progression;
- births/deaths/guardianship where causally due;
- household persistence and migration;
- no named-registry quota forcing;
- latent demographic substrate remains available for replenishment.

#### 5. Resolution

Record all:

- latent -> structural/tracked materializations;
- actor/institution promotions;
- demotions/down-resolution;
- institution candidate formation/crystallization.

Verify every resolution transition has causal evidence and preserves continuity.

Do **not** require a new gang/merchant house to form if no causal conditions produce one.

#### 6. Belief / information firewall

Sample strategic decisions and verify:

- no actor consumes canon/hidden truth as personal knowledge;
- reports respect travel/transmission delay;
- contradictory versions may coexist;
- competence changes interpretation rather than information access.

#### 7. Economy / production / regional development

Audit actual causal chains across:

- merchant records/offers/contracts/credit/shipments;
- craft dependencies and shortages;
- markets and prices;
- agriculture/harvest/food pressure;
- taxation/public finance;
- infrastructure/security/migration response.

No prosperity/development score may become an authority.

#### 8. Cultivation / education / institution reproduction

Check:

- training and teaching progress only through valid owners;
- Realization and Stage ecology remain heterogeneous;
- no hard population quota snaps Stage distribution back into place;
- institution training/reproduction continues off camera.

#### 9. Underworld

Check the concrete v0.60 model:

- organizations/crews/rackets/protection/fronts/economic interests;
- directed loyalty/fear/resentment where used;
- enforcement/heat;
- formation candidates where evidence exists;
- no automatic gang -> Mafia conversion;
- protection remains a racket with accounts, not a peer ontology to “racket”.

#### 10. War / health / law / agriculture

These owners must remain live even if the particular year is peaceful or low-event:

- field campaign mechanics must be callable/scheduled without inventing a war;
- health capacity and condition progression remain separate;
- agriculture produces only at actual harvest due times;
- law cases require claims/evidence/procedure and do not self-create merely to show activity.

### Failure conditions

ONEYEAR-01 fails if any of the following occurs:

- required subsystem never scheduled because it was forgotten;
- unexplained required-domain block persists;
- hidden omniscience crosses the belief firewall;
- tracked physical value is minted/destroyed outside its owning rule;
- catastrophic database integrity/FK violation;
- player/focus privilege changes simulation availability;
- dormant/latent world freezes;
- a resolution transition fabricates a biography/history instead of preserving source context;
- a domain has to be manually poked simply to keep ordinary world time moving.

### Acceptance output

Produce:

1. ONEYEAR-01 acceptance report.
2. machine-readable opening/end fingerprints and periodic diagnostics.
3. process-coverage summary.
4. causal anomalies / fixes made during the run.
5. a clean post-run engine checkpoint **separate from the diagnostic campaign save**.
6. only after acceptance, set/record `one_year_unattended_acceptance = passed` in the accepted gate fixture/checkpoint mechanism.

## Then — TENYEAR-01

Only after ONEYEAR-01 passes should `ten_year_gate()` open.

TENYEAR-01 tests compounding, recovery, institutional turnover, demographic replacement, economic/regional divergence, multi-generational continuity and long-run resolution behavior. It must not be used to discover that a major subsystem never started.
