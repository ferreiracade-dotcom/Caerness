- retaliation tied to grievances;

Full personal estates remain a later generic civilian/economic owner rather than an underworld-only system.

## ProcessRegistry status

ARCH-01 is **proved but not cut over**. `advance_connection` remains the current production path.

Do not confuse a successful spike with a completed scheduler migration. Revisit cutover when the event/domain-owner architecture makes the benefit concrete.

## Cross-domain direction remains unchanged

After the semantic foundations, proceed through vertical slices rather than isolated mini-games:
- merchant/market shock;
- craft/production;
- backwater count/state capacity;
- warfare;
- health/agriculture/law;
- integrated one-year run;

## Player-facing rule

Deep simulation remains engine-facing. The player acts through human intentions, people, projects, appointments, contracts, advice and risks rather than manipulating route weights, solver variables or market coefficients.
