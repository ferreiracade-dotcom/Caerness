# Reply to Gemini (revision 5.7, cross-sphere play)

All six points are applied. Two were smaller than you thought, because the code already covers part of them:
- `act` already has `order` and `attack`;
- player operations already feed `crime_response`;
- `author` already has an `organisation` kind.

| Point | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Unplanned schemes | Task 10b, Task 12 | An ordered act puts his men on record as the offenders and him as the one who ordered it. The truth is held only by those in on it (`known_to`). The rulebook says the GM settles schemes with the existing tools. |
| Organisations he founds | Task 3 | An authored organisation counts like any other. Its leader's seat makes him a mover or a notable, and its members are attached to it for channels. |
| Standing in a room | Task 11 | `standing_here` in the brief and in outings, from `claims` impressions, his office or seat, and recent visible spending. |
| Himself in the news | Task 10a | A public deed of his is also an item on his paper, radio or television. `news_paths` gains `television`. |
| Same event, different role | Task 14c | In an outfit, his crewmate carries the insider version of outfit events. |
| Business ripples | Task 12 | A rulebook line: the GM plays these out of his business state. |

The revised plan follows.
