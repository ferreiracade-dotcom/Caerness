## 2. Rules That Govern Every Part

**Tags.** Fixed (a given). Open (left for the run or the Storyteller). Weather (a dated pressure, never an outcome). Anchor (a real fact). Tendency (how things usually go). Design (invented, with a range). Example (an illustration).

**Figures.** A real figure is marked [verified] with its source named, or [memory, to verify]. An invented figure is Design and carries a range. Money is net, not gross, in 1975 dollars unless stated.

**Period Mind (Part II section 0).** Everyone thinks and judges as a person of 1975. Narrating a cruelty is not endorsing it.

**Content limits.** No real person acts or speaks. No method for making, moving or hiding any drug, weapon or explosive. No scripted plot and no named secret.

**Fixed structure, not reopened.** The four-family structure, the geography, the Part IV rules and the Part V dial defaults.
