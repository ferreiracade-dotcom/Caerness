# Culture and Standing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** How people react to the protagonist and to each other, and how his own House treats him, follows canon and engine facts: the GM sees each present person's standing in words, the room's canon manners, and the engine weighs standing (respect, fear, kin, household authority) when people decide.

**Architecture:** Canon [1610] puts facts in the engine and humanisation in narration, so most of this is surfacing facts the engine already stores. New `engine/standing.py` derives per-observer standing in words (age band, Stage reading with the regional offset, station, kin tie, household role, relationship band, recognition, origin/outsider). New `engine/manners_canon.py` holds compact paraphrased etiquette per region with locators. `connector/views.py` adds them to `present` and the scene; `engine/interactions._social_response` gains small standing terms. Rulebook gains a short culture section.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, SQLite, plain-script tests.

**Spec / sources:** `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-10-17-culture-canon.md` (binding for canon; locators `[N]`); connector spec §5 (no engine figures in prose), rulebook.

**Depends on:** run after gap 4a Task 1 (it edits `connector/views.py` and the rulebook). Independent of gap 4b; 4b's pay and loyalty later feeds household service loyalty.

## Global Constraints

- No numbers reach the GM for standing: words only ("a notable master by local measure", "your elder brother", "warm, trusts you", "does not know you").
- Canon has no Stage ladder or bow angles ([511], [512]); standing is several axes ([488]-[501]); Stage is not office, class or wealth ([1485], [2057]). Never collapse standing into one score shown to the GM.
- Regional Stage offset per notes §1.2 ([297]-[318]): outside the Hearth a Stage reads one step stronger (Hearth Third ≈ outer "strong master" impression, outer Sixth is a continental anomaly). Hearth membership from `cultivation.location_in_hearth`.
- Kin precedence outlives Stage ([551], [558], [1438]); fear raises formality but is not respect ([514]-[516]); kinship is not benevolence ([1438]).
- Resume budget ≤ 19000 chars still holds; scene growth per present person ≤ ~120 chars; manners line ≤ 300 chars.
- Never write `data/caerness.db`; tests on temp copies.

## Review Focus

1. A person with no relationship row, no Stage and no home still gets a sensible standing (e.g. "a stranger; ordinary"), never an error or "None".
2. The protagonist's own household: wife, children, kin, steward, servants are named as such relative to him ("your steward"), and household authority is never presented as obedience.
3. The Stage reading never leaks the Stage name of someone the protagonist could not plausibly read (Presence is not a scanner [352]): show the reading only where it is visible or known (known relationship, famous, openly displayed rank); otherwise "bearing of a trained fighter" style or nothing.
4. Standing terms in `_social_response` must not make kin or servants obey: obligations both ways; a cruel head loses them over time.
5. Outsider status uses the person's home polity vs the place's polity; people with no home are not called outsiders.

### Task 1: Standing in words (`engine/standing.py`)

**Interfaces:** `standing(conn, observer_id: int, subject_id: int, place_id: int | None) -> dict[str, str]` with keys (omitted when unknown): `age` (child/youth/young adult/adult/middle-aged/elderly/aged), `bearing` (Stage reading in words with regional offset, only if visible or known per Review Focus 3), `station` (office/role from `institution_memberships.role_text`, `underworld_personnel.role_kind`, `household_members.role`, political office), `kin` (relative to observer from `kinship_links`, incl. in-laws one step: "your wife", "your elder brother", "your wife's father"), `house` (household role relative to observer: "your steward", "a servant of your house", "of the Harrow household"), `toward` (relationship band from `relationships`: stance/trust/fear/respect/resentment → e.g. "warm; trusts you", "wary; fears you", "resents you"), `knows` ("knows you" / "knows of you" / "does not know you" from familiarity and, when present, `social_reputation`), `from` (home polity/settlement name and "outsider here" when home polity ≠ place polity).
- [ ] Tests (`tests/standing_test.py`, temp copy): a kin pair gets the right `kin` both ways; a household service term gives "your steward"; a hostile relationship gives a fearful/resentful band; an unknown person gives "does not know you" and no `bearing` unless they hold visible rank; a Hearth Third read outside the Hearth reads stronger than inside; elder sibling of a stronger younger sibling still "your elder brother". Fail, implement, pass. Commit `feat: standing in words`.

### Task 2: Canon manners as data (`engine/manners_canon.py`)

**Interfaces:** `MANNERS: dict[str, str]` keyed by culture (`hearth`, `churn`, `empire`, `court`, `dunmoor`, `greywood`, `windmere`, `sunreach`, `thornward`, `stormhollow`, `undertow`, `road`, `default`), each ≤ 300 chars paraphrased with a `LOCATORS` dict; `manners_for(conn, place_id) -> str` picks by the place's polity and Hearth membership (Hearth places may add the `churn` line when the scene involves Churn institutions; keep one line). Content from notes §1, §3 (e.g. Hearth: bow is grammar not a Stage calculator; hospitality before business; titles keep distance, over-familiar address insults; fear tightens formality; who does not become formal is revealing).
- [ ] Test: every key has text ≤ 300 and a locator; Toll's Reach → hearth line; a Dunmoor settlement → dunmoor line; unknown → default. Commit `feat: canon manners per region`.

### Task 3: Scene and lookup

**Files:** `connector/views.py`, `connector/rulebook.md` + spec Appendix A (identical), `tests/connector_views_test.py`, `tests/connector_resume_test.py`.
- `present[i]` gains the `standing()` words (merge keys; keep `id,name,sex,who,member_of`); scene gains `"manners": manners_for(...)` and `"you_here": {bearing/station as others here would read the protagonist}`.
- `lookup` of a person gains the same standing words; keep the existing rounded meters out of the GM's prose by rulebook (unchanged) — or drop them now that bands exist (decide and record).
- Rulebook, new short section "Standing and manners" (≤ 6 bullets): reactions follow standing, not one ladder; the scene's `manners` line is the room's grammar; kin and age precedence outlive Stage; deference is not admiration and fear tightens formality; his House has its own interests: kin and servants weigh how he treats them, a steward knows the business; regional manners beyond the line come from `lore`.
- [ ] Tests: scene `present` carries standing for a kin of the protagonist and a stranger; `manners` present; resume ≤ 19000. Commit `feat(connector): scenes show standing and manners`.

### Task 4: Standing in decisions

**Files:** `engine/interactions.py` (`_social_response`), `tests/interactions_standing_test.py`.
- Add to the score, for the recipient toward the proposer: `+0.20*respect/100` for requests/offers; `+0.25*fear/100` for demand/threat only (coercion already counts force; this is felt fear); kin: `+0.30` for requests between parent/child/sibling/spouse unless resentment ≥ 50 (then `-0.15`); household authority: if the proposer heads the recipient's household or holds an active service term over them, `+0.25` for orders/requests, scaled down by resentment; treatment memory: each `harm`/threat by the proposer toward the recipient in the last 365 days `-0.10` (cap -0.4). Clamp total standing contribution to ±0.6 so it never decides alone.
- [ ] Tests: same request to a kin vs a stranger with identical meters — kin accepts more often over 200 seeded trials; a resentful kin is not more compliant than a stranger; a household servant accepts an order more often than a stranger but not always (< 95%); fear raises demand compliance but not request compliance. Existing `interactions_coercion_test`, `player_operations_test` still pass. Commit `feat: standing weighs in decisions`.
