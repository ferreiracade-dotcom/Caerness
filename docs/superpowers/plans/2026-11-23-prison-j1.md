# Prison J1: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A person who is convicted lives on in a prison with its own groups, economy, visits and letters; their standing and family outside decay or hold with the quality of their channels; parole, appeal and release run through the existing law; the outfit and family carry on. NPC and protagonist alike.

**Architecture:** The case chain already exists (B13: report, case, arrest, bail, disposition, `sentences`, `custody`). J1 adds a layer beside it and does not fork it: `engine/prison.py` (facilities, stays, inside groups, weekly pass), `engine/prison_channels.py` (visits, letters, grip on the outside), `engine/prison_review.py` (parole, appeal, pardon, release). The inside groups are org-core organisations of one new kind (`inmate_society`) whose seats exist only for notable inmates; everyone else is a headcount. `crime_response` hands release over to J1 when the package has prison data. Everything with a world word lives in `worlds/saintjude/prison.json`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, SQLite, plain-script tests in `tests/` (`PYTHONPATH=. python tests/<name>.py`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-prison-design.md` (binding). Read with it: `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-19-saintjude-org-core-constraints.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

## Global Constraints

- No hard caps, floors, quotas, cooldowns or clamps to hit a number (owner, 2026-11-21). Targets are reported, never enforced; a result that is implausible is fixed in the driver. `engine/` has no world words: no facility, group, rank or place name and no dollar figure; they are data in `prison.json`, every figure `{"value", "mark": "Design proposal", "why"}`.
- The protagonist lives under the same rules. Every test of a system acting on people has a pair that acts on the protagonist (`engine.player.set_player`). One exception only: where his own survival is genuinely uncertain, lean toward living. No player-only rule; tests grep for it.
- Determinism: every draw is `random.Random(f"{run_seed(conn)}:prison:{stream}:{key}")` or `org_motives._roll`.
- New behaviour runs only when the package has prison data and `system_enabled(conn, "outfits")`. Caerness is ignored (no regression work), but `engine/` stays free of world words and `tests/no_world_words_test.py` scans the new modules (Task 1 adds them to `scanned_files`).
- Tables: `ensure_tables` in the new modules (idempotent, like `crime_response`) through Task 8; the schema migration and `schema.sql` hunks come last (Task 9) because another session (R1) has uncommitted edits in `engine/db.py` and `engine/schema.sql` (schema 127 is R1's). J1 takes **128**. Message R1 before touching either file, and stage only J1's hunks (`git apply --cached` of a diff limited to those hunks), never R1's.
- Tests clean up: `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory`, and when run directly set TMP/TEMP under `runs/` and remove it. After any test run `git restore game/campaign_01/model_requests/`.
- Never write `data/*.db`; leave `data/caerness.pre-schema110.db` and `game/campaign_01/backups/` alone. Commit with `git add <paths>` only, "J1" in the subject, never push, stash, reset or switch branches. Python: `"$LOCALAPPDATA/Programs/Python/Python313/python.exe"`.
- Long runs: at most two at once across J1 and R1, never alongside the quick suite, progress printed each simulated year, never the same seed twice.
- Don't edit R1's files (real-facts/history modules, `veto.real_person`).

## Review Focus

1. **A man convicted who sits in two organisations** (a capo who owns a firm): both seats go through held decisions with the same channel grip; the inside seat is a third seat that `_held_decisions` ignores. Pinned in Task 5.
2. **A cooperator** (he talks): protective custody, a separate group, lost standing outside, and the inside groups that would kill him cannot reach him easily. Pinned in Task 3.
3. **A co-defendant pair in the same facility**: two men of rival outfits in one block; the pass must draw conflict between them from their tension, not by a rule. Pinned in Task 3.
4. **Time-skip over a hearing**: a parole hearing or release day inside a 30-day skip is processed on its day (`next_due_day`), not at the end. Pinned in Task 6.
5. **Sentence vacated on appeal while he is mid-stay**: stay closed, custody back to `bailed`, case back at disposition, no dangling inside seat. Pinned in Task 6.
6. **Death or escape inside**: the stay ends, the sentence ends or becomes `escaped` with a new report, the inside seat is retired and the head succession runs. Pinned in Task 3.
7. **Facility with no room for a class** (intake rules match nothing): falls to the package's declared fallback facility; a missing fallback is a loud error, not a silent default. Pinned in Task 2.

## Tables (created by `prison.ensure_tables`; moved to schema 128 in Task 9)

- `facilities(id, key UNIQUE, institution_actor_id, location_id, name, level, security, capacity, headcount, status, character_json, metadata_json)` — `character_json` holds the drifting drivers (crowding, violence, corruption, contraband).
- `facility_groups(id, facility_id, group_key, org_actor_id, headcount, character_json)` — the headcount of everyone in the group, named or not.
- `inmate_stays(id, actor_id, facility_id, group_id, sentence_id, case_id, since_day, until_day, status, protective, block_key, metadata_json)` — status `inside`, `transferred`, `released`, `escaped`, `dead`. One `inside` stay per man.
- `prison_channels(id, stay_id, kind, outside_actor_id, quality, watched, cost_weekly, status, since_day, metadata_json)`.
- `prison_contacts(id, day, stay_id, channel_id, kind, from_actor_id, to_actor_id, summary, intercepted)`.
- `prison_events(id, day, facility_id, actor_id, other_actor_id, kind, severity, summary)` — conduct record the reviews read.
- `law_reviews(id, kind, sentence_id, actor_id, filed_day, hearing_day, status, outcome, factors_json, metadata_json)` — `kind` is `parole`, `appeal` or `pardon`; status `pending`, `granted`, `denied`, `withdrawn`.
- `inmate_ties(actor_a_id, actor_b_id, facility_id, since_day, kind, strength)` — bonds and enmities made inside; carried out at release.
