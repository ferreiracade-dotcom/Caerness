## 6. Why the treasury is 0 (answered first, it explains most of Q1)

Evidence:
- `underworld_heat` has no row for org 12 (rows exist for 57, 58, 59 etc.). The only monthly tick for organizations is `process_due_rebuild_underworld` (`engine/underworld_rebuild.py:2253`), which selects `underworld_organizations JOIN underworld_heat` (line 2254); `next_rebuild_underworld_due_day` (line 2248) joins the same way. An org without a heat row is never selected, so it never runs `autonomous_organization_turn` (2224): no `collect_monthly_income` (1705), no membership turnover, no crew reconcile, no heat review, no leader draw.
- Run A, 180 days: org 12 treasury 0.0 at every 30-day checkpoint, 0 finance rows, 0 operations. Same world: 254 of 255 other outfits end with treasury > 0 (avg 18.6, max 80.7). Stone Boars (gross rackets 23.2/30d) 54.4, Tin Hammers (8.6/30d) 25.4. Fane's rackets gross 21.5/30d, about the same as Stone Boars.
- Run B (heat row added, nothing else): treasury 8.21 after 30 days, 44.1 after 180 (+8.1/month; gross kick-up 10.2/month, overhead 1.26, leader draw 0.71). So routing is fine once the tick runs: kick-up goes to `underworld_organizations.treasury`, leader draw goes to `underworld_leader_wealth.liquid_wealth`, which is what `ledger.balance(("person",me))` reads as his purse (`engine/ledger.py:41`). It does not go to business cash or the purse by mistake, and the protagonist's org is not specially excluded.
- Cause of the missing row: the owner's hand-built setup script (`source: owner_protagonist_setup`, not in the repo) used `create_underworld_organization` (`engine/underworld.py:202`), which does not create a heat row. Heat rows are created by `ensure_heat_profile` only in the seeders (`underworld_ecology.py:187, 360`), by `engine/authoring.py:370` (the `author` tool does it correctly), and lazily by `add_heat`/`_review_heat`. `tests/fixtures_protagonist.py:17 make_boss` has the same hole, so tests built on it also have an org that never ticks.
- Accidental repair: any `add_heat` call creates the row (`underworld_rebuild.py:1666`). Run C: the first `attack` or open `demand` by Fane (`player_operations.py:531, 720, 722`) created it, and after that his treasury rose 8.2/month. So the first violent act in play would silently "turn on" the outfit.
- Opening treasuries: every outfit starts at 0. `treasury REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` (`schema.sql:2378`); the seeders (`underworld_ecology.py`) never set one, and all 255 are 0 at day 0 in the packaged world. So there was no "other outfits got a seed" difference: everyone starts empty and fills from kick-ups; Fane just never got the first kick-up. Note this also means a new outfit starts with no reserve and recruits/retains poorly (`underworld.py:487` uses treasury per full member / 6).

Smallest fix (two lines of intent):
1. Make the tick not depend on a heat row. In `create_underworld_organization` (`underworld.py:202`) call `ensure_heat_profile`, and add a one-off backfill in the next schema migration (`db.py`): for every `underworld_organizations` row with status in ('active','strained','fragmented') and no `underworld_heat` row, insert one with `next_review_day = current_day + 30`. (Alternatively `LEFT JOIN` in 2248/2254 plus `ensure_heat_profile` inside the loop; the creation fix is cleaner.) Add the same call to `tests/fixtures_protagonist.py`.
2. Opening treasury scaled to income and size, same for all outfits: at seed/backfill set `treasury = round(3 * monthly_net, 2)` where `monthly_net = (racket_gross*kick_rate + protection_gross*0.78) - overhead - tribute`, i.e. three months of what `collect_monthly_income` would net (it is computed there; factor a `monthly_net_estimate(conn, org_id)` out of it). For Fane that is about 3 x 8.2 = 25 (membership 25 full+peripheral, 3 rackets, 21.5 gross), comparable to Tin Hammers' 25 after six months and below Stone Boars' 54. Apply only where `treasury = 0` and there are no `underworld_finance_transactions` for the org (keeps live saves that have already earned money untouched). For Fane's live save a one-time `UPDATE ... SET treasury=25` plus the heat row is enough.

Related finding: the three Fane businesses' cash is not feeding anything. Run A: Silkwater (105) cash 38.5, Green Table (107) 50.1, Counting Room (108) 71.8 after 180 days, all accumulating in `civilian_enterprises.cash`, no owner draw (grep for owner draw/dividend in `business.py`, `civilian.py`, `merchant.py` finds none); his household (id 264) liquidity went 10 -> 6.2 over 180 days (`civilian.py:375` upkeep, no income). The rackets' "gross" is an estimate and is never subtracted from the business cash (`collect_monthly_income` comment, line 1740). So his two real purses (person, household) have no income at all other than the 8% leader draw once the org ticks.

## 1. 180-day advance: what involves the protagonist's outfit

Run A (as is), 180 days: org 12 appears in 0 operations, 0 incidents, 0 relations, 0 zone claims, 0 finance rows. Whole world in 180 days: 603 operations, all of kinds `delegate_racket` 288, `invest_in_racket` 256, `expand_protection_account` 26, `promote_personnel` 31, `truce` 2; 0 incidents; 6 relation rows total (3 neutral, 1 rival, 2 truce); 0 challenger claims; the only underworld events are 223 `underworld_membership_turnover`. Nothing aggressive happens anywhere in the world, not only against Fane. Run B (heat row fixed): same, Fane now earns and makes one `invest_in_racket` (via underboss 905, who counts as a leader in `_leader_org`, `underworld_decisions.py:18`; the player himself is skipped at `simulation.py:280`), and still 0 incidents/relations/claims.

Why nothing reaches him, in order:
1. No tick (Q6).
2. **The code that makes outfits attack is not called by anything.** `_territorial_attack` (`underworld_rebuild.py:1894`), `_targeted_hit` (1973), `_take_vacant_turf` (1882), `_open_new_racket`, `_buy_front`, `_patrol`, `_recruit` have no callers (grep over engine/connector/tests). `autonomous_organization_turn` (2224) says strategic choice moved to DEC-01 and removed them. But DEC-01's `underworld_decision_candidates` (`underworld_decisions.py:33`) only offers: reply to a `protection_poach` incident (47-77), `seek_truce` for existing hostile relations (80-91), discipline a crew with loyalty < 48 or cohesion < 45 (96), promote, delegate racket, `expand_protection_account` onto an *unprotected* enterprise in a zone the org already controls (141-155), invest. There is no challenge/attack/hit/occupy-vacant-turf/poach candidate. `underworld_candidate_actions` (`underworld_rebuild.py:2170`), which does enumerate `challenge_organization` and `occupy_vacant_turf`, is called only from `tests/v60_phase2a_test.py`.
3. Even if they were offered, they need ground: `_adjacent_targets`/`_vacant_adjacent_zones` (1787, 1800) start from the org's own controlled zones. Fane holds none (`underworld_zone_claims` has no row for org 12; Toll's Reach zones 50 River Quays = North Foxes, 52 Warehouse District = Tin Hammers, 53 Greyhook Dock = Stone Boars; 51 Sea Quays, 54 Caravan Quarter, 55 Market Quarter are vacant and stay vacant for 180 days because nobody calls `_take_vacant_turf`). Zone adjacency exists (366 rows; 50-51-52-53-54-55 ring), so reachability is fine once he holds a zone.
4. `expand_protection_account` picks `NOT EXISTS active protection account` (`underworld_decisions.py:146`), so rivals never poach an already-protected business; the only producer of a `protection_poach` incident is `protect_business` (`underworld_rebuild.py:1013`) when the *player's side* calls it, and no player operation calls it.
5. Seeded fact worth using: each Fane business already pays a Toll's Reach rival under an "opening_account" protection account (`underworld_protection_accounts`: Green Table 107 -> Stone Boars 57, Counting Room 108 -> Tin Hammers 58, Silkwater 105 -> North Foxes 59, all `disputed=0`), alongside `underworld_business_links` rows making them Fane "controlled_front"s. So the rivals take money from his businesses while his own rackets claim them. Nothing models that conflict.

Smallest hook points:
- Give him ground at day 0: `set_zone_controller` on a vacant zone (e.g. 55 Market Quarter) or `add_zone_challenger` on one, exactly what `authoring._claim_ground` (`authoring.py:329-340`) does; then rivals' adjacency lists include him.
- Add DEC-01 candidates built from rows that already exist: from `_adjacent_targets` -> `challenge_organization` (execute with the body of `_territorial_attack`), and from `_vacant_adjacent_zones` -> `occupy_vacant_turf`. Reuse the existing functions; add the action kinds to `execute_underworld_action` (`underworld_decisions.py:186`).
- Make hostility exist so the existing `seek_truce`/`_targeted_hit` paths have something to read: only 6 relation rows exist. `ensure_relation` is lazy; call it for every pair of orgs sharing a city in the seeder (`_ensure_relations_from_borders`, `underworld_rebuild.py:1488`, only handles bordering zones in `seed_city_underworld`, not the schema-110 ecology seeder).

Smallest hook: in `_attack` (after line 712) and in the refused/accepted branches of `_demand`, resolve the victim's outfit and insert one `underworld_incidents` row, plus `change_relation`. Concretely:

## 3. Pay

(a) Underworld personnel and crews: **no wage or payroll**. `collect_monthly_income` docstring and line 1743: "does not pay every member a central salary ... intentionally not payroll". Money to people exists only as: rackets' gross is treated as the members' earning (kick-up share `.34 + formality/100*.23` goes up; the rest is implicitly theirs); `overhead = 10% gross + 0.10 per crew + 0.08 per front` leaves the treasury with no payee; `leader_draw = 8% of net` to `underworld_leader_wealth` (`underworld_rebuild.py:1770-1776`); tribute upward. `pay_member_household_support` (977) and `pay_bribe` (958) exist but have no callers. `underworld_personnel.earning_power` is a static number (set at creation, `create_personnel` ~800). Treasury effect on people is only indirect: `simulate_underworld_membership_turnover` (`underworld.py:461`) uses `treasury / full_members / 6` as `economic_support` (34% weight of `success_support`) to lower exit rates and raise recruitment; with 25 members a treasury of 150 is "fully paid". Inactive at treasury 0 for Fane because the tick never ran (Q6); with treasury 0 and ticking it would use `economic_support=0`, giving the worst retention but no discrete event.
Cadence: monthly (heat clock, `next_review_day=day+30`). Insufficient funds: nothing happens (no payroll to fail). Fane treasury 0 with 25 members: confirmed there is nothing charged; he is unpaid-by-design and also not ticking.

(b) Business employees: **no wage line**. `enterprise_staff` (`schema.sql:4133`) has role/authority/specialty/status only, no pay, loyalty or satisfaction. Labour cost is folded into `operating_cost = gross*cost_ratio*efficiency + capacity*0.035*time` (`business.py:355`), charged to business cash monthly; a shortfall becomes `debt`/`strained`/`insolvent`/`closed` (`business.py:373-410`), staff are not touched. `_hire` into a business (`player_operations.py:169`, `_install` 224) adds an `enterprise_staff` row with no pay terms; `amount` is only the acceptance incentive passed to `_offer` (~line 197), nothing is charged. `_pay` with "wage" in the purpose only labels the flow (line 93); it is a manual transfer with no obligation. The three Fane businesses have only himself as staff (owner).

(c) Household servants/stewards: **implicit upkeep only**. `civilian._resolve_households` (`civilian.py:364-385`): `monthly_upkeep = active_members*0.08 + staff_capacity*0.004 + hospitality*0.003`, deducted from `households.liquidity`; servants count as members. When liquidity < 0 the shortfall eats `wealth`, status `declining`, then `dissolved` at wealth 0. No income side. Run A: Fane household 264 liquidity 10 -> 6.2 in 180 days (about 0.63/month; empties in about 16 months, wealth 25 about 3 years more). Steward Dara Harrow (903) and servant Elin Pell (904) have `household_service_terms` rows (`add_service_term`, `household_life.py:500`) with no pay field.

Smallest hook: one monthly payroll step in `autonomous_organization_turn` after `collect_monthly_income` (outfit side) and one in `_resolve_households` (household side), both through `ledger.transfer` so they hit `_record_economic_flow` with `flow_kind='wage'`: outfit -> `size_estimate`-based number of named personnel, household -> each service term, business -> each non-owner `enterprise_staff` row. Record unpaid months in a new column (`arrears` on service terms/crews or a `metadata_json` counter) rather than a new table, and let arrears drive Q4.

## 4. Loyalty

Where it lives:
- `underworld_crews.loyalty` / `.cohesion` (created 60/60, `create_crew`, `underworld_rebuild.py:823-844`). **All 308 crews are exactly 60/60 after 180 days.** Changed only by `discipline_crew` (+5/+4, `underworld_decisions.py:214`) and crew splitting (`underworld_rebuild.py:2397`, `-0..5`). Read by: DEC-01 `discipline_crew` (candidate only if loyalty < 48 or cohesion < 45, line 96; never true, 0 discipline ops), patrol/delegate crew ordering (`_patrol` 1871, delegate 130).
- `underworld_organizations.cohesion` / `membership_stability`: set at creation, no `UPDATE` anywhere in `engine/underworld*.py`; feed turnover rates only.
- `underworld_personnel`: no loyalty column.
- `relationships.loyalty` (`relationships.py:49-90`): nothing in `engine/` passes `loyalty_delta` (grep), so it stays at its default; only read as context (`decisions.py:101`, `player.py:118`).
- `actor_drives.loyalty`: a personality trait, used for utilities (`underworld_decisions.py:41`) and for `_obedience` (`player_operations.py:854`); static.
- `household_service_terms.loyalty / satisfaction / renewal_bias` (50/50/50 on creation, `household_life.py:500`): no code ever updates them (`UPDATE household_service_terms` appears only for `review_day`, `status`, `household_id`).
- `resources.crew_loyalty` is a misnomer; it is the current full-member headcount (`underworld.py:542-547`).

What raises/lowers it: nothing except discipline (+) and splits (-). Not wages, not mistreatment, not his violence, not the treasury.

Consequence paths and whether they fire:
- Discipline of a weak crew: **never fires** (threshold never crossed).
- Desertion / defection to a rival / informing / theft: **do not exist as individual events**. The only analogue is anonymous `defection` in membership flows (17% of exits, `underworld.py:516-530`), whose destination is nowhere (no rival gains a member, nothing is informed). It does fire in run B for Fane (membership exits 5 in 180 days) once the org ticks. No betrayal path: grep for betray/informant/turncoat in `engine/` finds none outside text lists.
- Quitting service: see Q5 (fires, driven by constants).
- Order refusal: `_obedience` (`player_operations.py:835`) uses the static trait.

Smallest hook: a monthly `loyalty` drift in `autonomous_organization_turn` (crews) and `_resolve_households` / `_resolve_service_terms` (service terms): `loyalty += k1*(paid_ratio-1) + k2*(treated_well) - k3*arrears_months`. Consumers exist already: the crew discipline candidate (<48) and the annual service review score (`household_life.py:954`). For betrayal add one DEC-01 or turn-time check: crew loyalty < 30 and a neighbouring org exists in the same city -> move `size_estimate` to that org's crew (`reconcile_crews_to_membership` already rebalances to the membership stock), record a `defection` flow with `counterparty_organization_id`, and insert an incident `poaching`/`defection` row targeting the loser, which the extended incident query (Q2) picks up.

## 5. Servants quitting / service terms ending

Exists and fires. `_resolve_service_terms` (`household_life.py:947`, called from the household process at line 1133, due via `next` at 1070): at each term's `review_day` (start + 365 days) `score = 0.3*loyalty + 0.3*satisfaction + 0.2*renewal_bias + 0.2*household.cohesion`, minus 20 if the household is `declining`; renew for another 365 days if `score >= 45` and the household is open, else the term goes `former`, the `household_members` row goes `former`, and a `service_change` life event is logged. Also ended by death (`mark_actor_deceased`, line 589), by the player's dismiss (`player_operations.py:272`), and re-pointed on household moves (479).

Verified by run D (service terms forced due at day 20; household liquidity 0 and wealth 0.5): both terms ended `former` at day 20, household became `dissolved`, members `former`; also term 1 with loyalty/satisfaction/renewal 10 would score 21 < 45 and leave even in a healthy household. With the live defaults (50/50/50, cohesion 65) the score is 51 > 45, so the live steward and servant will renew until the household declines or dissolves, which with no income happens in roughly 1.3 years from liquidity 10 (then `declining` costs -20 and both leave at the next annual review).

Gaps: annual granularity only (no mid-term quitting); nothing lowers loyalty/satisfaction when unpaid or mistreated; no theft, no informing, no "steward with high loyalty stays when household is declining"; business `enterprise_staff` and outfit `underworld_personnel` have no term/quit mechanism at all (`status` 'inactive'/'former' exist in `enterprise_staff` but only the player's dismiss sets them).

Smallest hook: add an arrears check in `_resolve_households` (liquidity went to 0 this month => `satisfaction -= 6` on each active service term) and make `_resolve_service_terms` also run monthly for terms with `satisfaction < 30` (change the due date query at 949/1070 to include them), so a mistreated servant quits within a couple of months instead of at the annual review.

## Suggested order of work for gap 4b
1. Heat-row creation + backfill + opening treasury (Q6): unblocks everything; two small edits and one migration.
2. Give Fane a zone (or make `author`/setup do it) and add `challenge_organization` / `occupy_vacant_turf` to DEC-01 candidates (Q1).
3. Player ops write an incident + relation and DEC-01 reads them (Q2); make `retaliate` actually hurt.
4. Monthly payroll through `ledger.transfer` with arrears counters (Q3), then loyalty drift and the defection/quit consequences (Q4, Q5).
5. Owner draw from business cash to purse/household so someone has money to pay with (Q6 related finding).

Side notes: (a) the engine's underboss (905) already spends org treasury on `invest_in_racket` for the protagonist's outfit once it ticks; probably wanted, but note he acts for the player. (b) Hiring into an outfit through `_install` calls `create_personnel` but does not touch `underworld_membership_profiles`, so the aggregate member count (8/17/10) and named personnel drift apart.
