# Hard-coded limits audit (2026-10-17)

Read-only audit of everything the GM can reach: the connector tools (`connector/*.py`), the engine paths behind `act` (`engine/player_operations.py`, `interactions.py`, `actions.py`, `ledger.py`, `timeskip.py`), `engine/authoring.py`, and `connector/rulebook.md`. `domain_actions.py` and `decisions.py` are NPC-only. Nothing in `connector/` imports them.

Judged against the owner's principle (the engine is a veto: it refuses only a canon rule, an established fact or world-scale implausibility, and names the contradiction) and the later rule (an oversized or implausible request gets what is possible plus the reason, not a flat refusal).

Classes: **A** hard limit on GM or player, must go. **B** internal category is fine, but free input is not mapped, or part of the input is silently dropped. **C** legitimate veto, already correct. **D** all-or-nothing refusal where a partial result, a counter-offer or a longer process is the plausible answer.

Proofs were run on temp copies of `data/caerness.db` with `tests/fixtures_protagonist.make_boss`, calling `connector.acting.act`, `connector.authoring.author` and the views directly. Scripts are in `...scratchpad/limits/probe1..6.py`.

## C: legitimate vetoes, already correct

- Draw rights and ownership (`ledger.can_draw`, `trade` "not his to sell", `_authority`).
- A dead or non-existent target, a missing id, "not enough" naming the purse (but see D1 for partial).
- Authoring: names unique and following sex, no kin or union against canon rules, no money, ownership or authority granted, the protagonist and his people excluded (`_not_his`), leaders already leading, savepoint all-or-nothing.
- Pending-interaction check for `respond`; `call_id` idempotency.
- The obedience roll in `_obedience` (NPC agency, not a menu).
- `advance` clamped to 30 days with `remaining_days` returned.

6. Starting size and strength constants for outfits, and the rumour constants (#24), are not mentioned.
7. The refusal `reason_kind` (#15) is not in the plan's Task 7 rulebook bullet.

## Notes

- Seven of the limits above (#2, #3, #4, #5, #6, #9, #10) share one fix. A single free-form operation takes the intent, the people and resources committed, the target and the time. It validates against `can_draw` and means, scales the outcome with the real manpower and money, and returns what was done plus why the rest was not (the new rule). The rulebook change (#15) is the cheap one and should land first.
- #1 is a silent disconnection of the kind AGENTS.md warns about. Fix it first: until then nobody can leave the starting town through `act travel` on the packaged world.
- Test gaps that let these through: `connector_act_test.py` runs on `seed_demo`, so it never saw an empty `location_links`; no test sends a free-form `details` key and asserts it is echoed or stored.
