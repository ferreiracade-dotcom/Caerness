# Caerness Civilian Life Depth — v0.17

## Purpose

v0.16 established the civilian simulation substrate. v0.17 makes that substrate accumulate institutions, property, style, succession and obligation over time. The goal is not a management game. It is a world in which peaceful lives leave durable history.

## Built environment

Buildings persist independently of the project that created them. They can belong to people, households or institutions and carry condition, prestige, utility, capacity, style and maintenance burden. Architectural projects may crystallize directly into buildings when completed. Maintenance is resolved coarsely through available support rather than room-by-room simulation.

## Styles and fashion

Styles exist as historical movements rather than item tags alone. Adoption is local and responds to prestige, novelty, accessibility, elite support, craft capacity and resistance. This allows court dress, merchant taste, architectural revivals and workshop traditions to spread at different rates in different places. Cultural objects can be linked to one or more styles.

## Succession and inheritance

A household can record a succession method, designated heir, certainty and dispute pressure. Competing claims can exist with different legitimacy, priority and social support. Succession only resolves from persisted conditions or an explicit manual choice. When personal cultural property changes hands through succession, provenance records the inheritance.

## Credit and partnership

Credit agreements are durable obligations. Principal, outstanding balance, periodic interest, due date, risk and default state persist. Enterprise borrowers receive the loan proceeds and debt; later interest changes the balance sheet. Partnerships separately record ownership share and governance weight so money and control do not have to be identical.

## Professional institutions

Professional memberships model the social life around careers: guild-like bodies, colleges, workshops, merchant institutions, scholarly circles, medical bodies and other professional organizations. Seniority accumulates and standing may make a member eligible for review. The engine does not auto-promote a person simply because enough days elapsed.

## Compression rule

> **Persist the institutional consequence; compress the routine that produced it.**

The engine should remember that a hall was built, a style spread, a debt defaulted, a household passed to an heir, or a professional became eligible for office. It does not need to narrate every maintenance payment, committee meeting or ordinary working day.
