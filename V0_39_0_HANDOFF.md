- Chemical medication/intoxicant/stimulant dependence, gambling dependence, and elixir performance dependence are separate causal channels.
- True Vein-Root elixir is never treated as chemically addictive by default.
- Psychological stressors are separate from persistent psychological conditions; ordinary distress is not automatically pathologized.
- Severe/repeated burden may create persistent trauma/grief/anxiety/depression/nightmare/obsession/etc. state.
- Family/religious/peer/medical/institutional support can help or harm without imposing a modern in-world diagnostic vocabulary.
- Persistent functional impairment feeds ordinary actor pressure; cultivation is not a cure.
- Autonomous ingestion currently covers concrete elixir exposures, severe health events and relationship-grounded bereavement.
