## Required RESOLUTION-01 proof inside COVERAGE-01

The earlier design decision remains binding: entities can move both directions across resolution levels for causal reasons.

Prove at least these cases:

### Person promotion

```text
aggregate/latent ordinary person
→ event or accumulated significance
→ lightweight/named tracked person
→ crystallized/rich actor if necessary
```

No requirement that every promotion traverse every intermediate tier if a causal event justifies a jump.

Examples to prove:

- ordinary merchant becomes regionally significant;
- background retainer becomes relevant through war/politics;
- witness/criminal/craftsman becomes named because a consequential event selects them.

### Institution/organization promotion

- a new gang or Mafia family reaches scale/territorial/economic significance and becomes explicitly tracked;
- a growing enterprise/institution can promote from aggregate ecology to persistent owner;
- no player/focus privilege.

### Down-resolution

Prove that formerly rich/tracked actors or organizations can safely down-resolve when their expensive detail no longer matters, while preserving:

- identity/lineage;
- important relationships;
- property/obligations;
- event/history provenance;
- aggregate contribution.

Death is not a special exception: dead people can retain only the historical state needed by surviving systems rather than remaining permanently expensive because they were once crystallized.

## After COVERAGE-01 — integrated ordinary one-year unattended run

Only after the gate passes, run one ordinary year with all major domains active at their appropriate resolution.

Required properties:

- the protagonist/player is not required;
- no subsystem waits for camera attention;
- no domain receives protagonist privilege;
- no scripted requirement that dramatic events occur;
- quiet outcomes are legal when causes support them;
- shocks/conflicts are possible where their conditions occur;
- coverage telemetry proves every required domain actually ran or had an explicit causal no-work reason.

The year should allow, but not force:

- births/deaths/marriage/household changes;
- training/cultivation progression and plateaus;
- business creation/closure/trade/credit/production;
- harvest/weather variation and food stress;
- illness/injury/treatment/capacity bottlenecks;
- legal complaints/procedure/enforcement;
- public finance/projects/migration;
- new criminal organizations or underworld escalation;
- political succession/conflict;
- war/campaign consequences;
- information/rumour/reputation propagation;
- promotion/down-resolution.

Acceptance is causal coverage, not a checklist demanding one example of every rare event.
