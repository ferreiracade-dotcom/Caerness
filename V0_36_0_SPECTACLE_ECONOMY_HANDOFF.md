## What v0.36 adds
- Aggregate local betting books, including Red Board footprints and independent books so the Red Board is not treated as a monopoly.
- Event-linked betting markets for tracked tournament bouts and structural Closed-Core prizefight cards.
- Public odds derived from public reputation/form only; hidden cultivation capability is not read into published prices.
- Aggregate settlement that changes bookmaker enterprise cash without inventing named bettors, bettor debt, or individual wagers.
- Structural professional Closed-Core prizefight markets. Cards run on latent fighter pools rather than manufacturing named adults.
- Persistent local form/injury load and incumbent-champion streaks.
- Promotion from structural champion to a one-person `latent_entities` identity after durable repeated success. Promotion stops at latent resolution; crystallization into a named person remains a separate causally-triggered process.
- Legal/tolerated prize halls are explicitly separated from coercive pits, fight fixing, fraudulent credit betting and criminal enforcement, which remain later underworld concerns.
