## Dedicated v0.33 regression
The test proves:
1. Vein-Root stock is consumed exactly once during refinement.
2. Batch replay cannot duplicate refined output.
3. False provenance claims do not mutate actual provenance.
4. Clean ordinary assimilation adds reserve without Realization, Sediment, or automatic Rupture.
5. Suspect product can add Sediment without automatic Rupture.
6. Gross clean-dose overload can cause Rupture.
7. Closed Core is not treated as diseased but cannot use Open-Core cultivation-fuel assimilation.
8. Heavy Sediment reduces later cultivation progress without erasing Stage.
